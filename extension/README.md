# Meeting Intelligence extension

Live assistance and optional email recaps for Google Meet. Zoom browser capture remains a preview pending a live-call check; Zoom desktop is not supported.

Join your meeting, open Meeting Intelligence, allow microphone access, and choose **Start listening**. Choose live assistance, an email recap, or both in Settings. Recaps are off by default. Jira tasks require your review and confirmation.

**Stop listening** releases your microphone. Rejoining the same room within ten minutes can continue the recap. Choose **Finish & email recap** when finished.
